# What can Ringbot do — and how fast can you get started?

**Ringbot** is an AI-powered voice agent and call center automation platform built for real business workflows.

In just a few minutes, businesses can use Ringbot to:

*   **Build and deploy AI voice agents** for inbound and outbound calls
*   **Create AI chatbots** powered by the same knowledge base
*   **Use AI agents** for sales calls, lead qualification, and phone surveys
*   **Answer customer enquiries** using natural voice and conversational AI
*   **Book appointments** and trigger real-time workflows
*   **Embed AI chatbots** directly into their websites

Ringbot is designed so teams don’t need long setup cycles or complex infrastructure. Voice agents and chatbots can be created quickly, trained with knowledge bases, and deployed across multiple channels to handle high-volume conversations at scale.

The platform is built for small and mid-sized businesses, sales teams, and modern call centers that want to automate conversations efficiently while keeping a human-like experience.
